## Downlink 1.1.1

Free video and audio downloader for Mac and Windows. Paste a link, download.

### New
- **Subtitles.** Embed them in the video, or save a separate `.srt` file.
- **Reddit and Instagram.** Works with public posts.
- **Passwords.** Password-protected Vimeo videos ask for the password.
- **Show the page.** If Downlink can't find a video, open the page yourself to get past a captcha or login. It finishes as soon as the video plays.
- **History.** Re-download or find past downloads, with thumbnails.
- **Clipboard.** Offers to download a link you just copied. Off, on focus or always, in Settings.
- **Updates.** Downlink now updates itself. It downloads quietly and shows "Update ready"; Settings > Updates turns it off.

### Simpler
- Always downloads the best quality, up to 4K. No quality menu.
- One switch, "Convert to H.264", shown only when the best video isn't already H.264.
- The cover image is always embedded.
- ProRes conversion is gone.
- New dark look.

### Under the hood
- Electron 45, with the latest security fixes.
- The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple.

### Install
- **Mac:** Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or later. Open `Downlink-arm64.dmg` and drag Downlink to Applications.
- **Windows:** 64-bit, Windows 10 or later. Run `Downlink-Setup-x64.exe`. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.
